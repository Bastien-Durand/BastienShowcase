    FAQ

    Before you ask

    Anything else, message me on WhatsApp.

    Do I need to be technical?

    No. Most of my clients aren’t. I explain everything in plain English, and you sign off on every change before it goes live.

    What does it cost?

    Every project starts with a free 30-minute call. After that you get a fixed-scope proposal, so you know the price before any work starts.

    Who do you work with?

    Mostly owner-led health, fitness and sport businesses (clinics, gyms, martial arts academies, clubs), plus startups that need a product built and taken to market.

    What is AI search, and why does it matter?

    People now ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Google’s AI who to call. Those tools recommend businesses they can find clear, trustworthy facts about. I make sure that’s you, then track what they say every month.

    Is it safe to use AI with our customer data?

    It can be. I map where your data goes, keep sensitive details out of AI tools, give each person only the access they need, and keep a human reviewing the decisions that matter.

    What happens after the project?

    You own everything: accounts, code and content. You get a playbook so your team can run it, and I’m on WhatsApp if you need me.
